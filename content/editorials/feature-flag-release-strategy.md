Agree on stable cohort membership and who can stop the pilot, then name the evidence that would drive that decision. The recovery rule needs to distinguish an open quote from a newly created quote. Calling the flag a quick rollback without trying both paths leaves an open question about money already shown to customers.

Spend seven minutes comparing included and excluded fixture accounts and nine rehearsing disablement with an open quote. Read the cohort outcomes at the API. Exercise the rollback through the UI so you can compare the price the customer sees before disablement with the confirmation amount. The decoy repeats successful pilot cases. Those cases can pass while an excluded account receives the pilot formula or disablement changes an open quote.

Set the criteria first, rehearse on the candidate, run the small pilot, then decide whether the tagged pilot data supports expansion. An excluded account receiving pilot pricing stops expansion immediately; expected quote continuity does not.

See [ISTQB CTFL, product risk control](https://istqb.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/ISTQB_CTFL_Syllabus_v4.0.1.pdf). An interviewer is looking for a named decision owner and a rollback rehearsal with a measurable result.
